\subsection{Distribution Shift, Reliability, and Selective Prediction}
A forecast can be well calibrated on historical validation data yet become unreliable when the input distribution or error process changes. Shift detection and selective prediction provide complementary mechanisms: the former identifies departures from a reference regime, whereas the latter allows a predictor to abstain when estimated reliability is insufficient. The central empirical requirement in this paper is that any reliability score must be validated against realized error and risk--coverage behaviour rather than justified only by intuition.
